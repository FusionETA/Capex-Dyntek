# Capex App — Simple Demo Script

A short, easy walkthrough (~10 minutes). One story: **someone asks for money to
buy something → the right manager approves it → Finance updates the sales numbers.**

---

## Get ready (before you start)

- Log in to **Bitrix24 as Rachel** (Group CFO). This account can do everything, so you
  can show the whole demo without switching users.
- Open the **Capex** app (left menu → **Capex**). Start on the **Dashboard**.
- Pick one number to use as your example amount: **40,000**.

**Open with this line:**
> "This is one place inside Bitrix where staff ask for capex, the right manager approves
> it, and Finance keeps the sales targets up to date. No spreadsheets, no email chains."

---

## Step 1 — The Dashboard (what everyone sees)

Point at the screen and say:
- "Up top: how much capex is approved, how many are waiting, and how many regions."
- "Here are the **sales targets** per region, with progress."
- "And here's the **approved capex, ranked** — one shared view everyone can see."

> "This is real data from your planning sheet — Singapore, Hong Kong, Malaysia, Indonesia."

---

## Step 2 — Ask for something (New request)

Click **+ New request**. Fill it in as you talk:
- Title: **Warehouse racking upgrade**
- Region: **MY**, Amount: **40,000**, Currency: **SGD**
- Add a PIC and a one-line reason.
- Click **Submit request**.

Say:
- "As soon as it's submitted, the system works out **who needs to approve it based on the
  amount**. This one is under S$50k, so it goes to the **HOD**."

---

## Step 3 — Approve it

Click **Approvals**. Say:
- "An approver only sees the requests **they** are allowed to decide."
- Click **Approve**.
- "Done — it's approved and now shows on the dashboard."

Then show the safety net (the moment people remember):
- "Now a big one." (Have or make a **S$2,000,000** request.)
- "As an HOD I **can't** approve this — it's over my S$50k limit, so the system stops me.
  It would need the CFO."

> "So people can only approve up to their limit — the rules are enforced, not just written down."

---

## Step 4 — Finance updates the target (Carol)

Click **Sales Targets**. Say:
- "After capex is approved, Finance updates the region's sales numbers here."
- "Carol **types in** the new target and what's been achieved. Nothing is auto-calculated —
  it's exactly what Finance signs off."
- Change **MY**'s number, click **Save**.
- Go back to **Dashboard** — "and it updates for everyone."

---

## Step 5 — Who can do what (access)

Say: "Not everyone should see everything, so **access is off by default**."

Click **Manage Access**:
- "This is the list of exactly who can use the app, and their role." Click the little **ⓘ**
  — "here's what each role means."
- "To add someone, pick them and choose a role, then **Grant access**. To change or
  remove, it's one click. No IT needed."

(Optional) If you have a second login:
- Open the app as someone with **no role** → "They get a clean **No access** screen."

---

## Step 6 (optional) — Show where the data lives

Only if the audience is technical or asks "where is all this stored?"

Open **CRM → Capex Request** (the board):
- "Everything you saw is stored in Bitrix itself, as normal Bitrix records."
- "Here's the request we just made — and when we approved it, it moved to the **Approved**
  column. Same record, one place."
- "There are only **two lists**: the capex requests, and the sales targets. That's it."

Say:
- "So there's no separate database and nothing hidden. If you ever remove the app, all
  these records **stay in Bitrix**."

---

## Wrap up

Say it back in their words:
- "One place in Bitrix — no spreadsheets or email approvals."
- "Requests go to the right person automatically, by amount."
- "Finance owns the sales targets, and everyone sees the same current picture."
- "You control who has access, and manage it yourself."
- "It's working and tested today. Going live on your system is the same setup."

---

## If they ask

- **"Is this separate from Bitrix?"** → "No, it *is* Bitrix. The data lives in Bitrix. If you
  ever remove the app, the data stays."
- **"Can we change the approval limits?"** → "Yes — we set them to your policy."
- **"Who can submit?"** → "You decide — usually your Tier 0–2 (MG–MS) staff."
- **"Do approvers get notified?"** → "That's the next step — an email and a task when a
  request comes in. The approving itself already works."

---

## After the demo

If you created a test request, delete it from the Bitrix **Capex Request** board, and put
the **MY** sales number back if you changed it. (Or ask us to reset the test portal.)
